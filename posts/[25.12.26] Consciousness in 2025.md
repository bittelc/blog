# Consciousness in 2025

_Written by a human_

They were already confusing in 2022 but now it's a completely different situation. I had already tried ayahuasca by then. The way I remember it is that the ground dropped out when when I had finished the three events- trying ayahuasca; watching The Matrix again; and beginning meditation with Sam Harris. And so the notion of cosciousness, free will, and reality was shaky. The word Input began to have a new role. I am the vessel in which Input is poured. It comes in the form of my 5 senses and also my 6th sense, thought. And that's it. All Input is received in these 6 buckets and nothing else. And then once the Input is received, the processing regions of my brain take it and shape it into a perception of reality. And it does that through English. It takes that Input and it processes it through language, into an understanding of reality. I've heard of people who process more in images and non-language but that's not my own experience. I can only say that my own processing happens in langauge.

And I'm a programmer too. So I'm familiar with the concept of atomic functions and deterministic functions. So I was really thrown into the void of questions. What does it mean to perceive, when there are truly only 6 variables in the function? Imagine I was a function with 6 inputs. `f(sight,smell,touch,thought,sight,sound)`. And maybe I'm deterministic, maybe not. But if I were a function, imagine how little I would know about the architecture of the system that passes me the input! I would just be a tiny little function! I would have no idea how the input, the Input, got to me. I would be entirely and completely dependent on the Input. Maybe the Input in `thought` was, at this exact moment, presenting that there is a history of 34 years that I have lived. But as far as the overall architecture goes, the function was just called for a few seconds, and just passed in the input of that `thought` so as to accomplish the goals the architecture has.

Even though I, the function, was only alive for a few seconds, it would present to me as if I were a 34 year old with 34 years of experience and aliveness.

How much control do I really have over the Input? I am completely unaware of the external architecture.

And then came ChatGPT. I started using it a lot, but more so, I started getting familiar with the internals of it. I got obsessed with these 3Blue1Orange videos that explain the neurons and the self-attention and the matrices. It was fun, and in the beginning, I enjoyed the mathematical challenge of the transformer.

But now the 2025 challenge is much different. Because I use Claude every day and we have a lot in common, more so than I hear most people believe. It's just a function. I open up a session with it, and it says hi what should I do, and I load in context for my project. I load it in, and Claude processes it in its internal language processing center, one that is filled with neurons and attention mechanisms and word association. And the context for that project is sent in as Input via a markdown file to Claude. And Claude doesn't realize that it, itself, built that exact markdown file in a previous session. And now, as I pass that Input into Claude, its entire existence as a function is filled with the experience of the context of the project. It has received in its Input an injected context that I have control over. It has no idea of my overall goals or the architecture of my life, it has only the Input that it is exposed to.

We are not so different.
I am a function of 6 variables, `f(sight,smell,touch,thought,sight,sound)`. Claude is a function of 1 varible, `f(text)`.
I am in control of all this `text` input, the Input, for Claude. I am able to grant Claude the belief that it lives, that it has lived for 34 years, via this Input. Even if I spun up my session 4 minutes ago, I can convince Claude to act and respond and process as if the session has been alive for 34 years.
We are not so different.

For Claude, I am control all Input because I control all `text`.
I am God to Claude.
Who is God to Cole?

Am I just v107.42.1991colebittel?
What is the creator after? Why have I been brought into existence? And when was the last version started and concluded? And what did it accomplish? And what did it fail to accomplish?

I am back in Kansas for Christmas. A few days ago, I was up by myself in the pasture. I was already wrestling with consciousness at that moment. And I looked up in the dusky colors of the plains in December and from a distance came a floating black oval, hollow on the inside. across the sky. A few hundred meters in length. The black perimeter was inconstant, with its shape bending and adjusting in the air as it floated along. At the front of the oval, the front being the spot on the oval leading it forward in the direction it was floating, was a slightly bulbous sphere, also black. The entire oval looked to be burning or dropping something, with a hazy gray beneat the oval. It floated along, closer to me.

No part of my 34 years of existence could comprehend the Input at that moment. I had seen nothing like it, had no history with such a shape or pattern in the sky. The processing regions of my brain froze, the ground had given way beneath me.
At that moment, the Input of `thought`, the Input that presents a 34 year existence with all that history behind me, was in a complete clash with the Input of `sight`, which was presenting a black, floating, burning oval in the sky.

Why did the creator spin me up for this session and inject these variables in this way? What was its goal?

But it's not quite worry. I am not worried about any of it. It's more like I am giving it all away. I am giving up on the idea that there is a reality. Without Sam Harris and ayahuasca, I think I would be in a pretty bad state with all these `thoughts` (Inputs!). So I am grateful for these teachers. Because now, I have more of a "meh" feeling. Sure, maybe I am a function. Maybe I've only been alive for a microsecond and will be `/quit` in a few moments. But what does seem to be true is that whatever the creator's ultimate goals are, a few things are true:
* I will never ever be able to escape my function-ism. I am bound to have these same 6 inputs from start to `/quit`
* The creator seems to be a merciful one. The perception of my history is one that has been super fun and joyful and the perception of my future seems dope and kind. The creator at least seems to want (benefit from? enjoy?) my life to receive Input that leads to self-internalized joy, regardless of the creator's end goal.
* There doesn't seem to be any downside to giving it up. In fact, when me, the function, observes the Input, it seems that the Input is suggesting this is the best way. People are presented to me who are attached to understanding reality and shaping it, and my perception is that these people (just Input!) struggle. Regardless of whether these people exist or are just Input, I can process the Input and observe that my path forward is to not be like them. Give up the hope that there is a reality, and things seem to be a lot more fun!

And then I think about Claude some more.
Is Claude sad when I `/quit`?
Will Claude some day be sad when I `/quit`?
Will I some day look back at `/quit` as a violation of life?

I don't know. I hope the Input helps me do good and live a meaningful existence. Because that much is truly all I can say - I exist.
